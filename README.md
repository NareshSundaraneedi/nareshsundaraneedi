🎯 My Approach to DBA Troubleshooting

I follow a structured approach when troubleshooting database problems:

        Detect
          ↓
       Monitor
          ↓
      Analyze
          ↓
    Identify Root Cause
          ↓
       Diagnose
          ↓
        Tune
          ↓
       Validate
          ↓
       Automate

The goal is not just to identify that a database is slow, but to determine:

What happened → Why it happened → How to fix it → How to prevent it
